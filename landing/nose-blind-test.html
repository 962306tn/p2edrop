What is actually happening in your nose

It is called olfactory adaptation, and it is not a flaw. It is your nervous system doing its job.

Your brain is built to notice change, not constants. A smell that is always present carries no new information, so after continuous exposure the receptors stop reporting it upward. This is the same reason you cannot smell your own perfume by lunchtime, and the same reason a bakery worker stops noticing bread.

You are not failing to keep your house clean. You are failing to know whether it is clean. Those are completely different problems, and only one of them can be solved by scrubbing harder.

The fix is boring, and it is about droplet size

A hand trigger throws big droplets. They land on top of the weave, pool where they hit, and soak one patch. That is why you get a dark ring instead of an evenly treated cushion. It is also why you quit after four squeezes.

A powered nozzle takes the same liquid and breaks it into droplets small enough to drift down between the fibers, and light enough that the fabric never saturates.
