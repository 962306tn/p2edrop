Nothing gets soaked, so nothing has to dry.

This is the objection worth answering directly, because it is the one that stops people. Everybody has left a dark wet ring on a sofa arm and then sat next to it for four hours.

A mist that fine puts down a fraction of the liquid over a much wider area. The fabric goes slightly damp to the touch and is dry again in minutes. You can treat the sofa and then sit on it.
