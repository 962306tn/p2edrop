Don't take our word for it

Three tests you can run yourself.

No reviews to read, no percentages to trust. Run these on the first day and you will know what you bought.

Test 01
The stopwatch

Time one pass over a three-seat sofa. Then press a dry palm into the cushion. If it comes back damp, you have been sold a sprayer, not an atomiser.

Test 02
The tissue

Hold a tissue a foot from the nozzle for two seconds. A fine mist leaves it faintly cool and still readable. Droplets leave wet spots you can see through.

Test 03
The trigger count

Count the passes it takes to do one room, then check the pod. If a pod cannot cover your living room at least twenty times, the running cost is not what anyone told you.

Do all three inside 30 days. If any of them disappoint you, send it back and keep the pods.

Read this before you buy

What VelaHush is not.

It is not a cleaner. It will not lift a stain, and it will not do anything about the reason the stain is there. Blot the accident first, then treat the smell that stays behind in the padding.

It is not a disinfectant. It neutralizes odor. It makes no claim to kill anything, and you should be wary of a pet product at this price that says otherwise.

It is not a diagnosis. A sudden change in how your dog or cat smells, or in where they are going to the toilet, is a reason to call your vet, not to buy a mist.
