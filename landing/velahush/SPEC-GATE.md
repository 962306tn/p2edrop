## Rows 11 and 12 are the ones that matter

Row 12 has been answered as "safe for all". That is a conclusion, not the spec.
The spec is a yes or no: **does the solution contain essential oils, and which**.

It matters because 8.1's own note flags citrus, pine, eucalyptus and tea tree as
a problem for cats, and two of the four scents on sale are Lemon and Peppermint.
Until row 11 gives an ingredient list and someone qualified reads it, the page
does not answer the cat question at all. That FAQ has been removed rather than
softened.

One question to the supplier probably closes it:

> Is the scent from **natural essential oil** or **synthetic fragrance**?

"Fresh Linen" is not a plant, so the whole range is likely synthetic. If it is,
row 12 is No, the warning does not apply, and the cat FAQ can be written
properly.

## Policy vs measurement

The distinction that decides whether a number stays on the page:

- **Measurements** (rows 1-8, 13, 16) describe the physical object. Nobody can
  decide them. Until measured, the claim comes off.
- **Policies** (rows 17, 18, prices, guarantee) are set by the owner. They can
  be decided today, and they stay on the page as soon as they are.

That is why "90-day warranty" survived this pass and "5µm" did not.
