So you have been solving it with the wrong tool, not the wrong chemical

Look under your sink. There is a decent chance you already own something that works.

Enzyme cleaners are genuinely good chemistry. They break odor molecules apart instead of covering them. That part of the problem was solved twenty years ago, and you already bought the solution.

Here is what nobody put on the label.

What you tried Where it stops
Air freshener, candles, plug-insTreats the air. Two hours later the room smells like flowers that own a dog.
Hand-trigger enzyme bottleTreats one spot. Forty squeezes gets you half a cushion, a dark wet ring, and a sore hand.
Carpet shampooerGenuinely deep. Also two hours, a damp carpet all day, and it lives in the closet.
Professional cleaningEffective. Nobody books it four times a year.
Baking soda and vinegarFree. Also useless on a vertical sofa back.

Notice the pattern. Every one of them either treats the air, or treats a spot. And the smell is neither. It is spread thin across every soft surface in the room: the couch, the rug, the curtains, the dog bed, the blanket nobody has washed since spring.

A room-sized problem has been getting a spot-sized tool. That is the whole story.
